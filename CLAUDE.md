# Swerve Music website — house rules

These are hard constraints from the project owner. Follow them on every change, without being asked again.

## Never do these things

- No purple gradients (solid `--purple` fills only — gradients read as generic AI-startup styling)
- No pill-shaped buttons (the site's buttons are sharp-edged/rectangular by design; keep it that way)
- No fake reviews, testimonials, ratings, or star widgets
- No fake metrics or counters (stream counts, follower counts, "X fans joined" — anything not real and sourced)
- No vague hero copy ("Empowering the future of sound" style filler) — copy must say something concrete
- No emoji used as icons or decoration
- No em dashes (—) in any copy, anywhere. Use a period, comma, or the site's existing `·` separator instead
- No over-the-top scroll animations (the current fade + translateY reveal is the ceiling, not a floor to build on)
- No cursor-following effects (cursor glow/trail/blob). This was removed once already — don't reintroduce it
- No AI-slop photography or AI-generated copy — every photo and every line of copy must be real and supplied by the user
- No fake customer counters

## Launch blockers — do not treat this site as launch-ready until all of these are true

- [ ] Custom domain connected
- [ ] Favicon is the real Swerve Music mark (fixed — see `public/favicon-48.png`, `favicon-192.png`, `apple-touch-icon.png`)
- [ ] "Made with AI" badge/tag removed (check the hosting platform's settings — not present in this repo's source, so it's likely a platform-level toggle, not a code fix)
- [ ] Privacy policy page added
- [ ] Terms and conditions page added

## Working style

- Precision matters more than speed here. Double-check copy, links, and visual details before calling something done.
- When in doubt about whether something crosses one of the lines above, ask rather than guess.
